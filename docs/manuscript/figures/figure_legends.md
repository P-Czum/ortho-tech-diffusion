# Figure legends (material 48)

**Figure 0. Anatomy of an attention curve.** Share of field records containing the phrase, by year, for hip resurfacing, with every landmark defined in Box 1: baseline (mean share 2000–2002), threshold (max(5 × baseline, 0.1%) and ≥ 5 papers), emergence y₀ (first of three consecutive years above threshold), doubling time (log-linear fit from y₀ to peak), peak, persistence windows anchored to the technology's own peak (+1–3, +3–5, +5–7 years), and the decline criterion (share in 2025 ÷ peak share < 0.5).

**Figure 1. What emerged, and when.** The 48 technologies grouped by kind and ordered by year of emergence. Marker = y₀; bar = y₀ to peak year; an open arrow marks technologies still growing (peak in 2025). For two technologies attention peaked in a short spike before the first three consecutive years above threshold (mesenchymal stem cells, 2013; highly cross-linked polyethylene, 2008), shown as a dashed bar running left of the marker; microfracture peaked in its year of emergence (2012) and has no bar.

**Figure 2. Who started a technology, and who leads its literature.** For each country, the number of technologies whose earliest papers came from it (both origin measures agreeing; 36 of 48) and the number whose literature it leads (most papers from emergence onward, first-author affiliation). Twelve technologies whose two origin measures disagree have no "started" bar.

**Figure 3. Concentration is not paper count.** Effective number of countries (1 ÷ Σ p²) against the number of papers from emergence onward (log scale), kind by marker. The seven single-country literatures (effective number ≤ 2.5) are numbered and keyed with the leading country's share.

**Figure 4. Six kinds diffuse differently.** Doubling time (left, log scale) and effective number of countries (right) for each technology, grouped by kind; bars are medians. Doubling time is undefined for nine technologies (peak less than two years after emergence, or before it, or fitted slope not positive); these appear only in the right panel.

**Figure 5. Ten declines, relative to each technology's own peak.** Share ÷ peak share from three years before to seven years after each technology's own peak year. The two technologies whose evidence turned against use (metal-on-metal THA, hip resurfacing) in dark; the other eight in light. Curves end at 2025; the dual-mobility cup (peak 2020) has five post-peak years, so its +5–7 window is censored.

**Figure 6. A name is not a technology.** (a, b) Two retronyms: the series of the rival technology and of the name it created for the existing standard (single bundle after double-bundle reconstruction; mechanical alignment after kinematic alignment). (c) Four names of one technology — kyphoplasty, balloon kyphoplasty, percutaneous kyphoplasty, PKP — and their union, which is what the paper counts; the first name alone would read as a decline.
